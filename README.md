## 🕹️ About

Isekai: Waifu Overlord is an adult idle fantasy RPG about rebuilding a ruined village, restoring its economy, recruiting powerful waifus, and leading them into monster-filled dungeons. Manage buildings, grow crops, build relationships, and strengthen an automatically fighting party. This Windows snapshot is an unofficial leaked pre-release build that surfaced before the finished release.

For adult players who enjoy anime-style idle RPGs, village management, character collection, romance systems, and hands-off combat.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🏘️ **Rebuild the Village** — Upgrade farms, markets, taverns, smithies, and other production buildings.
- 💰 **Idle Economy** — Generate resources while offline and return to collect your profits.
- 💘 **Recruitable Waifus** — Recruit heroines, assign them to work, and assemble your ideal roster.
- ⚔️ **Automatic Battles** — Train, equip, and deploy your party against monsters and dungeon threats.
- 🎁 **Relationship System** — Give gifts, raise affinity, and unlock personal stories and adult scenes.
- 🌾 **Farm Management** — Plant, water, and harvest crops to complete village orders.
- 🌀 **Dungeon Expeditions** — Explore dangerous areas, clear monster waves, and pursue better rewards.
- ♻️ **Rebirth Progression** — Reset your current economy to gain permanent bonuses for future cycles.
